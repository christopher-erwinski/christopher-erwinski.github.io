01
AI Lead Qualification System
Real estate agency — Netherlands
Make.comClaude APIGmailGoogle Sheets
▼
The problem
The client was receiving 20–30 property enquiries per week through their website contact form. Every enquiry required manual reading, a judgment call, and a hand-written reply. This was taking 2+ hours daily and leads were frequently missed or replied to hours later — by which point they had contacted a competitor.
What I built
A fully automated lead handling pipeline: every form submission triggers Claude AI to score the lead as Hot, Warm, or Cold with a one-line reason. All leads log to Google Sheets. Hot leads send an instant alert to the agent. Every lead receives a personalised reply within 60 seconds, 24/7.
Results
  • 0 minutes/day spent on manual lead replies — was 2+ hours
  • Every lead answered within 60 seconds including evenings and weekends
  • Full lead pipeline visible in one spreadsheet with AI scores
  • Zero missed leads since implementation

02
AI Content Repurposing Pipeline
Marketing agency — Netherlands
Make.comClaude APINotion
▼
The problem
The client publishes 4 blog posts per month and wanted to repurpose each one across LinkedIn, Twitter, and their email newsletter. Doing this manually took 45–60 minutes per article — a 4–5 hour monthly task for one team member.
What I built
A one-click content pipeline: paste an article URL, Make.com fetches the content, Claude extracts 3 key insights and rewrites them as a LinkedIn post, a 5-tweet thread, and an email newsletter section. All three land in Notion within 30 seconds.
Results
  • Repurposing time from 45–60 minutes to under 2 minutes per article
  • Monthly saving: ~4 hours of team time
  • Team now repurposes every article — previously skipped some due to time
  • All content organised automatically in Notion by date

03
AI Customer Service Chatbot
Local service business — Netherlands
Make.comClaude APIGoogle DocsGmail
▼
The problem
The client received 15–20 customer questions per week via email — mostly the same questions about pricing and availability. Answering them took 1–2 hours per week, and questions outside business hours went unanswered until the next morning.
What I built
An AI assistant trained on the business's own information: customers submit questions via a form, Claude reads a Google Doc knowledge base and writes an accurate answer using only their real data. The customer receives a professional reply within 60 seconds. The owner gets a daily digest.
Results
  • 0 minutes spent on routine customer questions — was 1–2 hours/week
  • All questions answered within 60 seconds including nights and weekends
  • Answers specific to the business — not generic AI responses
  • Client updates knowledge base themselves — no technical knowledge required
